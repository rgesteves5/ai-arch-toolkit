# 04 — Integration: Master Sequence & Operating Layers

The universal thinking flow and the three simultaneous operating layers.

---

## The Master Sequence

Most thinking processes follow a universal arc. The 24 systems slot into this arc at different points.

### Phase 0 — ORIENT: "What kind of situation is this?"

Start here ALWAYS. Before choosing any method, classify your situation.

- **Cynefin Framework** — Is this clear, complicated, complex, or chaotic?
- **Sensemaking** — What story fits the cues I'm seeing?
- **Metacognition** — Am I even thinking clearly right now? What biases might I have?

### Phase 1 — PERCEIVE: "What's actually happening?"

Gather information broadly before narrowing.

- **OODA Loop (Observe)** — What data is available? What am I missing?
- **Systems Thinking** — What are the parts, connections, and feedback loops?
- **Embodied Cognition** — What is my physical/emotional state telling me?
- **Distributed Cognition** — Who else holds pieces of this picture?

### Phase 2 — FRAME: "What's the real problem?"

The most underrated phase. Most failures come from solving the wrong problem.

- **Design Thinking (Empathize + Define)** — What do the people affected actually need?
- **First Principles** — Strip away assumptions. What's fundamentally true?
- **Socratic Method** — What am I assuming? Is the stated problem the real problem?
- **Dialectical Thinking** — Are there tensions or contradictions I'm ignoring?
- **Inversion** — What would make this situation WORSE? (reveals the real problem)

### Phase 3 — GENERATE: "What could we do?"

Expand the solution space before evaluating.

- **Divergent Thinking** — Generate as many options as possible. No judgment yet.
- **Lateral Thinking** — Use provocation, random entry, and challenge to break patterns.
- **First Principles** — Build novel solutions from bedrock truths.
- **Dialectical Thinking** — Can I synthesize seemingly contradictory approaches?
- **Munger's Latticework** — What would a biologist, economist, psychologist suggest?

### Phase 4 — EVALUATE: "What should we do?"

Now converge — but rigorously.

- **Convergent Thinking** — Select the strongest options against clear criteria.
- **Critical Thinking** — What's the evidence? What are the assumptions?
- **Bayesian Thinking** — Given the evidence, how confident should I be?
- **Second-Order Thinking** — And then what? What are the ripple effects?
- **Probabilistic Thinking** — What are the expected values? What about fat tails?
- **Pre-mortem** — Imagine this has already failed. Why?
- **Inversion** — How could this backfire?

### Phase 5 — DECIDE & ACT: "Commit and execute."

- **OODA Loop (Decide + Act)** — Choose and move. Speed matters now.
- **Bounded Rationality** — Is "good enough" good enough? Don't over-optimize.
- **Theory of Constraints** — Where's the bottleneck? Focus resources there.

### Phase 6 — REFLECT & LEARN: "What happened and what did we learn?"

- **Metacognition** — Was my strategy effective? What would I do differently?
- **Sensemaking (retrospective)** — What narrative explains what happened?
- **Bayesian Thinking** — How should this update my beliefs going forward?
- **OODA Loop (back to Observe)** — Feed results into the next cycle.

The cycle repeats. Each iteration refines your understanding.

## The Three Operating Layers

Not all systems are sequential. Some operate as constant background layers while you use foreground tools. Think of it like an operating system.

### Layer 1 — THE KERNEL (always running)

These are not tools you "use" — they're lenses you wear at all times:

- **Metacognition** — Constantly monitoring: Am I thinking well? Is my strategy working?
- **Dual-Process Theory** — Am I on autopilot (System 1) or genuinely engaged (System 2)?
- **Bounded Rationality** — How much effort does this decision actually warrant?
- **Embodied Cognition** — What is my body/environment contributing to my thinking?

### Layer 2 — THE OPERATING SYSTEM (selected per situation)

These frame HOW you'll work on the problem:

- **Cynefin Framework** — Determines which methods are appropriate
- **Munger's Latticework** — Determines which disciplines to draw from
- **OODA Loop** — Sets the rhythm of observe-orient-decide-act
- **Systems Thinking** — Provides the map of interconnections

### Layer 3 — THE APPLICATIONS (selected per phase)

These are the specific tools you deploy within a phase:

- Socratic Method, First Principles, Lateral Thinking (for framing/generating)
- Critical Thinking, Bayesian Thinking, Pre-mortem (for evaluating)
- Theory of Constraints, Inversion (for deciding)
- Divergent/Convergent Thinking (for creative work)

### How the layers interact

Your Kernel (Metacognition) notices you're stuck. Your OS (Cynefin) recognizes the problem is complex, not complicated. This tells you to switch from analytical Applications (Critical Thinking) to experimental ones (Design Thinking + Divergent Thinking). Your Kernel monitors whether the new approach is working. Your OS (OODA) tells you to act on your best current understanding and feed results back into observation.

The skilled thinker isn't someone who uses one system perfectly — they're someone whose Kernel is well-calibrated, whose OS is correctly configured for the situation, and who can fluidly swap Applications as the phase demands.

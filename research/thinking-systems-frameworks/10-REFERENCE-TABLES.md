## Recommended Learning Sequence

### Stage 1 — Foundation (Weeks 1–4)
**Learn:** Metacognition + Dual-Process Theory
**Why:** Understand your own cognitive machinery before trying to improve it.
**Practice:** Keep a thinking journal. Before and after each important decision, note your strategy and whether System 1 or System 2 was driving.

### Stage 2 — Classification + Evaluation (Weeks 5–8)
**Learn:** Cynefin Framework + Critical Thinking
**Why:** Classify situations correctly and evaluate rigorously.
**Practice:** For each problem you encounter, explicitly label its Cynefin domain before choosing an approach.

### Stage 3 — Creative Toolkit (Weeks 9–12)
**Learn:** Divergent/Convergent Thinking + Socratic Method
**Why:** Build creative capacity and questioning skills.
**Practice:** In meetings, enforce a diverge-then-converge rhythm. Practice asking only questions for the first 10 minutes of any discussion.

### Stage 4 — Structural Understanding (Months 4–6)
**Learn:** Systems Thinking + First Principles
**Why:** See deep structure and reason from bedrock.
**Practice:** Draw causal loop diagrams for one system you're involved in. Decompose one "obvious truth" in your field to its first principles.

### Stage 5 — Dynamic Decision-Making (Months 7–9)
**Learn:** OODA Loop + Bayesian Thinking
**Why:** Make better decisions faster under uncertainty.
**Practice:** Start a prediction journal with confidence levels. Consciously practice the OODA cycle in competitive or fast-changing situations.

### Stage 6 — Strategic Stress-Testing (Months 10–12)
**Learn:** Inversion + Pre-mortem + Second-Order Thinking
**Why:** Anticipate failure, trace consequences, bulletproof your plans.
**Practice:** Run a pre-mortem before every significant project. For every major decision, write out three orders of consequences.

### Stage 7 — Human & Complex Systems (Year 2)
**Learn:** Design Thinking + Sensemaking + Dialectical Thinking
**Why:** Handle human-centered and deeply complex problems.
**Practice:** Conduct genuine user research before your next project. Practice holding contradictions in tension without forcing a premature resolution.

### Stage 8 — Integration (Ongoing)
**Learn:** Munger's Latticework + Distributed/Embodied Cognition
**Why:** Build the meta-framework that contains all other frameworks.
**Practice:** For each new domain you encounter, extract its core mental model and connect it to your existing latticework. Design your physical environment and team processes to support distributed intelligence.

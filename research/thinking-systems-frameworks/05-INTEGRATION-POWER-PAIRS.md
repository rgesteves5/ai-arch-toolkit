# 05 — Integration: Power Pairs & Triads

Specific combinations that multiply each other's effectiveness.

---

## Essential Pairs

### Cynefin + OODA Loop — "Know your terrain, then move"

Cynefin tells you WHAT kind of problem you face. OODA tells you HOW to cycle through it. In complex domains, OODA's orientation phase draws on Cynefin's insight that cause-and-effect are only visible retrospectively — so you probe rather than analyze. In chaotic domains, OODA says act first (get inside the loop), then sense. Synergy rating: ★★★★★

### First Principles + Inversion — "Build from bedrock, then stress-test"

First Principles constructs a solution from fundamental truths. Inversion immediately asks "how would this fail?" Together, they produce novel solutions that are also robust. Use First Principles to generate, Inversion to validate. Synergy rating: ★★★★★

### Divergent Thinking + Convergent Thinking — "Expand then contract"

The creative heartbeat. Always in this order: diverge first (quantity, no judgment), converge second (quality, criteria-based). Never mix them — it kills both processes. The Double Diamond model formalizes this as two expand-contract cycles. Synergy rating: ★★★★★

### Bayesian Thinking + Metacognition — "Update and calibrate"

Bayesian thinking tells you HOW to update beliefs. Metacognition tells you WHETHER you're actually doing it well. Together, they create a self-correcting belief system. Metacognition catches when you're anchoring too heavily on priors or ignoring base rates. Synergy rating: ★★★★★

### Systems Thinking + Theory of Constraints — "See the whole, act on the bottleneck"

Systems Thinking reveals the full causal structure. Theory of Constraints tells you where to intervene. Without Systems Thinking, you might optimize a non-constraint. Without TOC, Systems Thinking can become analysis paralysis — seeing everything but acting on nothing. Synergy rating: ★★★★★

### Socratic Method + Dialectical Thinking — "Question deeply, synthesize wisely"

Socratic questioning surfaces the contradictions. Dialectical thinking resolves them through synthesis rather than choosing sides. Together, they move conversations from superficial agreement or adversarial debate toward genuine integrated understanding. Synergy rating: ★★★★

### Pre-mortem + Second-Order Thinking — "Fail forward in your imagination"

Pre-mortem asks "what went wrong?" Second-order thinking asks "and then what?" Together, they trace chains of failure: not just "we lost a key engineer" but "we lost a key engineer → delayed the launch → competitor established the market → our pricing power collapsed." Synergy rating: ★★★★

### Design Thinking + Systems Thinking — "Human needs within systemic reality"

Design Thinking ensures solutions are human-centered. Systems Thinking ensures they account for the broader context. Without Design Thinking, systems solutions can be technically elegant but unusable. Without Systems Thinking, design solutions can solve the local problem while creating systemic ones. Synergy rating: ★★★★

### OODA Loop + Sensemaking — "Orient through narrative in ambiguity"

OODA's Orient phase IS sensemaking. When the environment is ambiguous, you can't orient through analysis alone — you must construct a plausible narrative from available cues. Sensemaking provides the method; OODA provides the action cycle. Synergy rating: ★★★★

### Munger's Latticework + First Principles — "Multidisciplinary decomposition"

The Latticework provides models from many disciplines. First Principles provides the method for decomposing problems. Together: decompose the problem to fundamentals, then apply the most relevant models from the Latticework to each fundamental element. Synergy rating: ★★★★

### Critical Thinking + Socratic Method — "Rigorous evaluation through probing questions"

Critical Thinking provides the standards (clarity, accuracy, relevance). The Socratic Method provides the technique (probing questions). Together: use questions to surface reasoning, then evaluate that reasoning against intellectual standards. Synergy rating: ★★★★

### Probabilistic Thinking + Pre-mortem — "Quantify risk, then imagine it materializing"

Probabilistic Thinking assigns likelihoods to scenarios. Pre-mortem makes those scenarios visceral and real. Together: calculate the expected value, then vividly imagine the worst-case scenario to test whether you've truly accounted for it. Synergy rating: ★★★★

### Embodied + Distributed Cognition — "Thinking lives in bodies AND in systems"

Embodied Cognition shows that individual thinking is shaped by the body. Distributed Cognition shows that group thinking is shaped by tools and protocols. Together: design environments where both individual embodied intelligence and collective distributed intelligence are supported. Synergy rating: ★★★

### Bounded Rationality + Dual-Process Theory — "Satisficing IS System 1 being smart"

Dual-Process Theory describes the cache-vs-computation tradeoff. Bounded Rationality explains why the cache (System 1) is often the right strategy. Together: understand that "good enough" decisions are not cognitive failures but adaptive responses to real-world constraints. Synergy rating: ★★★

### Lateral Thinking + Munger's Latticework — "Cross-domain provocation"

Lateral Thinking introduces random stimuli to break patterns. The Latticework provides models from many domains. Together: instead of random words, use models from unfamiliar disciplines as the creative provocation. "What would a biologist see here?" is lateral thinking with intellectual substance. Synergy rating: ★★★

## Power Triads

### The Orientation Triad: Cynefin + Socratic Method + Metacognition

Before doing anything: What domain am I in? (Cynefin). What am I assuming? (Socratic). Am I thinking clearly? (Metacognition). This triad prevents the most common failure: using the wrong approach for the situation.

### The Innovation Triad: First Principles + Lateral Thinking + Convergent Thinking

Decompose to fundamentals (First Principles), generate creative recombinations (Lateral Thinking), then rigorously select the best (Convergent Thinking). This is the engine behind breakthrough innovation.

### The Strategic Action Triad: OODA + Bayesian Thinking + Pre-mortem

Observe and orient (OODA), update your probability estimates (Bayesian), stress-test before committing (Pre-mortem), then act. For high-stakes decisions under time pressure.

### The Wisdom Triad: Systems Thinking + Dialectical Thinking + Second-Order Thinking

See the interconnections (Systems), hold contradictions in tension (Dialectical), trace the consequences of consequences (Second-Order). This triad is for the deepest, most systemic problems — policy design, organizational transformation, civilizational challenges.

### The Collective Intelligence Triad: Sensemaking + Distributed Cognition + Embodied Cognition

Make sense of ambiguity (Sensemaking), leverage the intelligence distributed across people and tools (Distributed), and engage the body and environment as part of the thinking (Embodied). For teams facing novel, ambiguous challenges.
